<p class="section-label">About</p>

I am a **Quantitative Finance Developer** with an M.S. in Applied Mathematics from [San Diego State University](https://www.sdsu.edu/). My work sits at the intersection of mathematical modeling, statistical computing, and financial engineering — building tools that turn rigorous theory into production-ready code.

My technical background spans stochastic calculus, numerical methods, machine learning, and systems programming, with hands-on experience in Python, C++, and R. I am particularly interested in:

- **Derivatives pricing & risk** — Monte Carlo simulation, finite difference methods, Greeks computation
- **Statistical arbitrage & factor models** — time-series analysis, PCA, cointegration strategies
- **ML in finance** — alternative data, regime detection, reinforcement learning for portfolio optimization
- **High-performance computing** — vectorized numerical code, parallel simulation, CUDA/GPU acceleration

---

<p class="section-label">Background</p>

Previously, I worked as an **IT Specialist at [Westcliff University](https://www.westcliff.edu/)**, where I designed data pipelines, managed cloud infrastructure on AWS, and built internal tooling for authentication and virtual desktop environments.

My graduate thesis, advised by [Dr. Uduak George](https://georgelab.sdsu.edu/) and [Dr. Joy Philips](https://sci.sdsu.edu/dpsbsc/), applied **spatial transcriptomics** to identify immune-function signatures in vaping-associated pulmonary injury — a project that deepened my expertise in large-scale data analysis, Bayesian inference, and bioinformatics pipelines. The same computational rigor translates directly to quantitative finance research.
