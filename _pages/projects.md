<div class="project-card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/anhdnguye/options-pricing-engine" target="_blank">Options Pricing Engine</a></h3>
  <p>
    A fast options pricing library implementing Black-Scholes, Binomial Trees, and Monte Carlo simulation for European and American options.
    Includes Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho) computation and an implied volatility solver via Brent's method.
  </p>
  <div class="project-meta">
    <span class="project-lang">Python</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Derivatives</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Monte Carlo</span>

    <span class="project-tag">Greeks</span>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/anhdnguye/portfolio-optimizer" target="_blank">Portfolio Optimizer</a></h3>
  <p>
    Mean-variance portfolio optimization with Markowitz efficient frontier, Black-Litterman model, and risk parity allocation.
    Backtesting framework with transaction cost modeling, drawdown analysis, and Sharpe/Sortino ratio reporting.
  </p>
  <div class="project-meta">
    <span class="project-lang">Python</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Portfolio Theory</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Optimization</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Backtesting</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Risk</span>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/anhdnguye/stochastic-vol-models" target="_blank">Stochastic Volatility Models</a></h3>
  <p>
    Calibration and simulation of Heston, SABR, and rough Bergomi stochastic volatility models.
    Finite difference PDE solver for option pricing under local vol surface. Includes volatility surface visualization tools.
  </p>
  <div class="project-meta">
    <span class="project-lang">Python</span>
    <span class="project-lang">C++</span>
    <span class="project-tag">Stochastic Vol</span>

    <span class="project-tag">PDE</span>

</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/anhdnguye/factor-model-research" target="_blank">Equity Factor Model Research</a></h3>
  <p>
    Multi-factor equity model inspired by Fama-French. Implements momentum, quality, value, and low-volatility factors
    with PCA-based factor extraction, covariance shrinkage (Ledoit-Wolf), and walk-forward cross-validation.
  </p>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/anhdnguye/ml-regime-detection" target="_blank">ML-Based Regime Detection</a></h3>
  <p>
    Hidden Markov Model and change-point detection algorithms for identifying bull/bear market regimes.
    Ensemble of Gaussian HMM, CUSUM, and LSTM-based classifier. Evaluated on S&amp;P 500 and VIX time series.
